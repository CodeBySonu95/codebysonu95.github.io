Privacy Policy for VoxTranscript

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoxTranscript. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the VoxTranscript Android application. We aim to be transparent about how the application processes your files and uses third-party services.

2. Audio and Transcript Processing

VoxTranscript is designed to perform AI transcription locally on your device using AI models downloaded and installed on the device.

Your audio files are not uploaded to our servers for transcription. The audio processing and transcription performed by VoxTranscript take place locally on your device.

Transcript text generated by the application is also processed and stored locally on your device.

VoxTranscript does not intentionally upload your audio recordings, audio content, or transcript content to developer-controlled servers.

3. Internet and Network Access

VoxTranscript may require an internet connection for certain application features, including:

  • Retrieving AI model information and download URLs.
  • Downloading AI model files from their respective download locations.
  • Using Firebase services for application functionality and analytics.

Internet access is not required for the actual AI transcription process when the required model has already been downloaded and the relevant functionality is available offline.

4. AI Model Downloads and Firebase Realtime Database

VoxTranscript uses Firebase Realtime Database to retrieve information related to available AI models, including model metadata and download URLs.

AI model files themselves are not stored inside the Firebase Realtime Database. The database is used to provide information and download locations required by the application.

When you choose to download an AI model, the model file is downloaded over the internet and stored locally on your device.

Your audio recordings and transcript files are not uploaded to the Firebase Realtime Database as part of this process.

5. Local Storage and File Access

VoxTranscript may use Android's file-access mechanisms to work with files selected or created by you.

The application may use local storage to:

  • Access audio files that you select for transcription.
  • Store downloaded AI models.
  • Delete downloaded AI models when you choose to remove them.
  • Save transcript files created by the application.
  • Read files that you explicitly select for processing.

These files remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share, export, upload, or transfer them using another application or service.

6. Firebase Analytics

VoxTranscript uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand basic application usage and improve the application.

Depending on the application's configuration and the Firebase SDK, analytics services may collect technical and usage information such as app usage events, sessions, device and app information, operating system information, and app-instance identifiers.

VoxTranscript does not intentionally send the contents of your audio recordings or transcript files to Google Analytics for Firebase.

Analytics information may be used to understand application usage, identify technical problems, and improve the application.

For more information about Firebase data practices, please visit Google's Firebase documentation and privacy information.

7. Information We Do Not Intentionally Upload

As part of the normal transcription functionality, VoxTranscript does not intentionally upload the following content to developer-controlled servers:

  • Your audio recordings.
  • The contents of your audio recordings.
  • The contents of your transcripts.
  • Your locally saved transcript files.
  • Your locally downloaded AI model files.

These files are processed or stored locally on your device.

8. Third-Party Services

VoxTranscript may use third-party services provided by Google and other service providers for specific application functionality. These may include:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Google Play Services

These third-party services may process technical information required to provide their respective services. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

For Google's privacy information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

9. Data Security

VoxTranscript performs AI transcription locally on your device. Your audio and transcript content does not need to be transmitted to our servers for the application's normal transcription functionality.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and locally stored files.

10. Children's Privacy

VoxTranscript is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws in their region.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as VoxTranscript evolves, new features are introduced, or the application's data practices change.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
