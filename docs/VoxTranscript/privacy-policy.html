Privacy Policy for VoxTranscript

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoxTranscript. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the VoxTranscript Android application.

2. No Account Required

VoxTranscript does not require you to create an account or log in to use the application.

We do not directly collect information such as your name, phone number, or email address through the application.

3. Audio and Transcript Files

VoxTranscript processes your audio and creates transcripts locally on your device.

Your audio files and transcript files are not uploaded to our servers as part of the normal transcription process.

Files created or used by the application remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or transfer them.

4. Offline Usage

VoxTranscript uses AI models that can be downloaded to your device. Once the required model has been downloaded, the main transcription functionality can be used offline.

An internet connection may be required to download or update models and for certain online services used by the application.

5. Local Storage

The application may use your device's storage to access audio files, save downloaded AI models, and store transcript files created by you.

6. Firebase Analytics

VoxTranscript uses Firebase Analytics to understand basic application usage and help improve the application.

Analytics is used for general usage statistics and application improvement. Your audio recordings and transcript contents are not intentionally sent to Firebase Analytics.

7. Third-Party Services

VoxTranscript uses certain Google services, including Firebase, to provide application functionality and analytics.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and its associated services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

VoxTranscript is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
