Privacy Policy for VoxTranscript

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoxTranscript. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the VoxTranscript Android application.

2. No Account Required

VoxTranscript does not require you to create an account or log in to use the application.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address through the application.

3. Audio and Transcript Files

VoxTranscript processes your audio, video, and voice dictations locally on your device.

Your audio recordings and transcript contents are not uploaded to our servers as part of the transcription or dictation process.

Files created or used by the application remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to share, export, or transfer them.

4. Offline Usage

VoxTranscript uses on-device AI models that can be downloaded to your device. Once a model has been downloaded, core transcription and dictation features operate completely offline.

An internet connection may be required to download or update models and for certain online services used by the application.

5. Local Storage

The application uses your device's storage to import audio or video files, save downloaded AI models, and store transcripts or subtitle files created by you.

6. AccessibilityService API Disclosure

VoxTranscript offers an optional automatic text insertion feature for floating voice dictation. If you choose to enable this feature after an explicit in-app disclosure and consent prompt, VoxTranscript uses the Android AccessibilityService API solely to detect the currently focused editable text field and insert your locally transcribed speech into other apps (such as chats, emails, and notes).

  • Limited Scope: The service only accesses the active editable text field and cursor or selection position to insert your dictated text properly. It never reads password fields or unrelated screen content.
  • Zero Data Collection: VoxTranscript does not collect, store, transmit, or share any screen content, keystrokes, or personal data accessed through the AccessibilityService API.
  • User Control: This permission is completely optional. VoxTranscript works without it (using clipboard copy or the Voice Keyboard), and you can disable the service at any time in your Android Accessibility Settings.

7. Microphone, Voice Keyboard & Floating Overlay

  • Microphone: Used only when you actively initiate recording or voice dictation. Audio is processed locally on your device.
  • Voice Keyboard: Processes voice input locally to insert text into the active app. It does not log, store, or transmit your typing or credentials.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Used strictly to display the optional Floating Voice Bubble on your screen for quick dictation access.

8. Firebase Analytics

VoxTranscript uses Firebase Analytics to understand basic application usage, app stability, and performance to help improve the application.

Your audio recordings, voice dictations, and transcript contents are never sent to Firebase Analytics.

9. Third-Party Services

VoxTranscript uses certain Google services, including Firebase, to provide application functionality and analytics.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and its associated services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

VoxTranscript is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
